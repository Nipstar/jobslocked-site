Straight answers

Will customers know it's not a person?

If they ask, it tells them. Most don't ask — they've got a problem in the house and they want someone booked. It sounds calm, gets the address, the problem, and a time, and stops there.

What happens on a real emergency?

It asks two questions, then rings you live. If you don't pick up, it books the earliest slot and tells the caller exactly when you'll be there.

Does it quote prices?

Only the ranges you give it, and only if you want it to. Default is "the owner will confirm on site." It never invents a number.

Can I keep my number?

Yes. We forward your existing number, or give you a new tracking number for ads. Either way your customers dial what they've always dialed.

How long until it's live?

Setup call, then about a week to build and test with your real job types. You hear it before a customer does.

Am I locked into a contract?

Month to month. The setup fee covers the build; the plan covers the running. Leave whenever it stops paying for itself.
